Dwarka Expressway Strategic Investment Guide

The Northern Peripheral Road (Dwarka Expressway) links Shiv Murti on Delhi's NH-8 to Kherki Daula toll plaza. Sectors 102 through 113 offer direct signal-free access to IGI Airport (Aerocity) in 15 minutes, the Diplomatic Enclave II, and the Asia's largest convention centre (Yashobhoomi IICC).

Sectors 102 & 104

Home to Emaar, Adani, Hero Homes, and BPTP. Established high-density residential clusters with operational schools & healthcare.

Sectors 106 & 109

Luxury enclaves including Godrej Meridien and ATS Tourmaline. Features upscale clubhouses, branded spas and fine dining.

Sectors 112 & 113

Direct Delhi Border frontage. Signature ultra-luxury addresses including Experion Windchants and Tata Gateway Gurgaon.
